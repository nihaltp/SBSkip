## SB Skip v{version}

### How to install

Download the APK for your device from the **Assets** section below:

- `app-arm64-v8a-release.apk` — 64-bit ARM (most modern phones)
- `app-armeabi-v7a-release.apk` — 32-bit ARM (older devices)

Then sideload the APK on your Android device. You may need to enable "Install from unknown sources" in your device settings.

---

SB Skip removes SponsorBlock-marked sections from your downloaded media automatically in the background.
